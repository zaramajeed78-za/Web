About My Movie Journey 🎬

Movies have always been my escape. Whether it's a mind-bending sci-fi thriller or a heartwarming drama, the right film can change your entire day.

Over the years, I've watched hundreds of movies, but a few stand out as absolute masterpieces. Inception made me rethink what a story could do. The Dark Knight showed me how superhero films could carry real emotional weight. Oppenheimer proved that history, when told right, can feel like a thriller. Each of these films shaped how I see cinema today — and this page is my small tribute to them.
